Privacy Policy — Kulliyat-e-Nazar

Policy date: 13 October 2026
App: Kulliyat-e-Nazar / کلیاتِ نظر
iOS/iPadOS bundle ID: pk.nazar.kulliyat
Developer/publisher: محمد تابش صدیقی
Privacy and support contact: mtabishs@gmail.com

This policy covers the offline literary reader Kulliyat-e-Nazar.

1. Data collection
No account is required. The app does not request access to your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ID or device identifiers, and does not send those details to the developer. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics service or remote-server data transfer feature.

2. Reading preferences stored on your device
The app stores favorite section identifiers, text size, light/dark reading preference and your last reading position in the app's local storage (UserDefaults) on your iPhone or iPad. These values support reading continuity and your selected settings. They are not sent to the developer or a third-party server. The app provides no cloud synchronization or backup feature. Text-search and glossary queries are processed on the device.

3. Sharing text with another app
When you choose to share, the poetry text is passed through the iOS share sheet to the app you select. That app may process it under its own privacy policy. Kulliyat-e-Nazar does not send the shared text to a developer server.

4. Security, retention and deletion
Local preferences remain in app-specific storage and are used for reading features. The reader opens only offline files bundled with the app and blocks external network requests. You can remove a favorite through the app. To delete all local preferences and reading position, delete the app from your iPhone or iPad. Local data is retained until the app is deleted. The developer holds no user account or remote reading profile to delete.

5. Children
The app has no registration or personal-information submission feature for any reader, including children. This statement does not assign the app an age rating.

6. Contact and changes
For privacy or support questions, contact mtabishs@gmail.com. If you choose to email us, the address and text you send will be received through email for that correspondence; this is not automatic collection by the app. Avoid including unnecessary personal details. If the app's behavior or this policy changes, an updated policy will be published here with a new date.
